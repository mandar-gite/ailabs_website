# Partner program eligibility: NVIDIA Inception and Claude Partner Network

Assessed 2026-10-11 for questionnaire Q19. Criteria come from third-party guides. Neither search returned an official Anthropic or NVIDIA page, so verify on the official pages before submitting.

## Company facts used

- Legal form: HUF (Hindu Undivided Family), as stated by the founder. Not an incorporated company.
- Team: 3 people, 2 developers.
- Website: 72ai.in, live.

## Verdict

| Program | Verdict | Blocker |
|---|---|---|
| NVIDIA Inception | Eligible on team, site and age. Open on legal form. | Stated requirement is an incorporated company. An HUF isn't one. |
| Claude Partner Network | Not now. | Team size, entity type, and tier thresholds. |

## NVIDIA Inception

Reported criteria:

- At least 1 developer on staff.
- A functioning website.
- Founded less than 10 years ago.
- Incorporated, with an incorporation date on the application. Revenue isn't required.
- No crypto companies.
- Free, no equity.

72 AI Labs meets the developer, website and age criteria. The entity question is unresolved, because the application asks for an incorporation date and it's unclear whether it accepts an HUF or proprietorship registration.

Next step: apply anyway, using the GST or business registration date, and describe the entity accurately. If rejected, an LLP or Pvt Ltd registration would clear it.

## Claude Partner Network

Reported criteria (sources conflict on details):

- Application: registered business, 10 or more employees, supported region, offering consulting, implementation or managed services. Anthropic decides acceptance.
- Registered tier: free, and several sources say no practitioner minimum. That conflicts with the 10-employee line above.
- Select tier: 10 active certified practitioners, 2 production joint customers, 1 public customer story.
- Preferred and Global Premier need 100 and 1,000 certified practitioners respectively.
- One guide lists an extra "Certified" tier (3 or more Architect or Developer certifications). Others omit it, so the tier ladder is unsettled.

72 AI Labs sits below the 10-employee line and well below Select. No guide mentions HUFs, and a program that vets consultancies may want a registered business entity. The free Registered tier is discretionary at best.
