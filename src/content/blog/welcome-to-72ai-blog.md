Welcome to the 72° AI Labs blog. This space is dedicated to sharing practical insights about AI implementation, real-world case studies, and operational intelligence specifically designed for Indian small and medium businesses.

## What to Expect

Our blog will cover:

- **Real Implementation Stories**: How businesses transform scattered data into actionable decisions
- **Technical Deep Dives**: Practical guides on RAG pipelines, OCR systems, and forecasting models
- **Industry Insights**: Specific use cases in F&B, manufacturing, and cross-functional SME operations
- **Frugal Engineering**: Building effective AI systems without enterprise budgets

## Why We're Writing

Most AI content focuses on either cutting-edge research or enterprise-scale implementations. We're filling the gap: practical, cost-effective AI systems that work for businesses operating at Bharat-scale realities.

Every post will prioritize:
- Clear ROI metrics (₹ saved, hours saved)
- Real code and architecture decisions
- Honest trade-offs and constraints
- Implementation steps you can follow

## Stay Updated

We'll be publishing new articles regularly. Each post tackles a specific operational problem and shows exactly how we solve it—no hype, no abstraction, just working systems.
